Merge Extras

Used in conjunction with and in addition to the Merges menu to manage controlled release of product. Can be used in conjunction with scanners to set up additional merges, to disable, or to fire a lane. A response from another menu (such as Triggers) will activate merge extras, in turn, responses from this menu are sent to control a motor.

Merge Extras are used in unique conditions that require an override of regular merges and decisions. Not all FortnaPlus© applications will employ the merge extras menu.
