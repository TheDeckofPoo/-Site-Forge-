Encoders

Optical Encoders are devices that interpret rotary motion on a shaft into electrical impulses, or signals. Encoders are used allow the control program to monitor how many times a shaft turned. When this informataion is used in conjunction with a timer, distance and speed of the conveyor can be calculated.
