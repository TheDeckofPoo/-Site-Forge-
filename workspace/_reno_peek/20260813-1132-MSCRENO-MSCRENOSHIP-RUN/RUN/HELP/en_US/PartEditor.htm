Part Editor


The Part Editor can work with single parts or with a group of parts.
  • To select a single part, click on it with the right mouse button.
  • When a single part is selected, you can edit text fields to change its graphics parameters: position, width, length, angle, etc.
  • To select a part group, click on the part you want to add to the group with the right mouse button while holding the Shift key.
  • To remove a part from the group, click on it again while holding the Shift key.
  • Also you can mark* a rectangular area of the conveyor screen by using the right mouse button, and all parts from the marked area will either be added to the existing group (if Shift is pressed) or otherwise will organize a new group.
  • To change position of a single part or group, you can also click on the single part or on any part of the group and drag it/them to the new location while holding the mouse button.
  • To align parts from the group, press the   button for horizontal alignment or the button for vertical alignment.

    *-to mark a rectangular screen area, click and drag the mouse pointer, then release. The start and end points define the area.
