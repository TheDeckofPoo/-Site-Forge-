Lift Gates Control

The Lift Gates Control monitors the status of lift gates on the conveyor line and the status of product moving on the line as it approaches the lift gate. If a lift gate is in the up position and product is close on the line, the control process stops the conveyor. Both conditions must be met for the conveyor to be halted. When the lift gate is returned to the down position, the control restarts the conveyor and sounds a horn (if equipped).

The following fields are in the Lift Gates Control menu:

  • Name - the internal name of the lift gate monitored by this control

  • LimitSwitch - the switch that monitors the state of the lift gate

  • PhotoEye - the photoeye that watches the section of the line immediately before the lift gate for approaching product

  • Motor - the motor that controls the conveyor to be stopped if the lift gate is up and there is approaching product

  • Horn - the horn to be sounded when the conveyor is restarted

  • Error - a flag generated by the FortnaPlus© program to indicate that an error exists

  • Status - a flag generated by the FortnaPlus© to show a stopped condition (indicated by a "1") or a normal condition (indicated by a "0")

  • Owner - the name of the CPU that controls the section of the conveyor system in which the lift gate resides.
