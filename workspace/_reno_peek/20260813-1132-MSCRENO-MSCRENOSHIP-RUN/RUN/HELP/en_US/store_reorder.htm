Re-ordering Stores

The stores for the day's work are order into waves in the original sequence delivered to FortnaPlus© from the HEB host. Any store that is in a wave, which has not been released, can be re-ordered. Usually, the store will exist in a higher (and therefore later) wave number and the desire will be for the store to be picked and palletized sooner in the day.

Using the left and right arrow buttons at the top of the screen, select a source wave and a destination wave.

When you arrive at the "Source Wave" desired, highlight the desired "Source Store" by clicking on it with the left mouse button. If you make a mistake, you may click on it again to de-highlight the store.

Alternatively, the store number may be entered into the "Selected Source Store" field at the bottom of the screen where the word "Search" is displayed. If the store is found, the wave display at the top will change to the wave for that store and the store number will be highlighted.

The goal is to select a Source Wave, a Source Store, and a Destination Wave. Then the partner clicks on the button. This will move the Source Store into the last position of the Destination Wave, and will move all other stores up to the Source Store up one position. The message box will state "Please wait, Re-Calculating Waves" until FortnaPlus© has re-assigned stations to all the stores remaining for the day. Of course, any wave, which has been North/South balanced, will need to be re-balanced.

Similarly, the partner may elect to move a store out to a later wave. The procedure is the same, except the goal is to select a Destination Store, Destination Wave, and a Source Wave. Note that the meaning of destination and source are reversed. The partner will then click on the button. The Destination Store will be moved to the first position of the Source Wave and all stores lower (earlier) than the Destination Store will be moved down one position. FortnaPlus© will again re-calculate the waves.

Should the partner wish to swap two stores positions, simply highlight the two stores to swap and click the button.

  • Press escape to return to the change store priorities screen.

  • Clicking on the button causes all stores to revert to the original HEB downloaded sequence.

  • Clicking on will bring up this help screen.

  • Click on to return to the main FortnaPlus© screen.
